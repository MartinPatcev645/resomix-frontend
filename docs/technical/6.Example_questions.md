6. Technical Answers for Rights Holders
When pitching to labels, the technical due diligence team will present a questionnaire. The following drafted responses synthesize the architectural improvements outlined above into industry-standard language.

Q1: How do you ensure the security and integrity of our pre-release content? Answer: "Our platform utilizes a secure, cloud-native ingestion pipeline compliant with DDEX ERN 4.3 standards. All content is ingested into an isolated object storage environment via encrypted channels (TLS 1.3). Immediately upon ingestion, assets are scanned using technology to verify copyright clearance before they are indexed. At rest, all media files are stored on private, non-publicly addressable volumes. Delivery is secured via AES-128 encrypted HLS streams with dynamic, session-bound token authentication that prevents unauthorized sharing or stream ripping."

Q2: What is your disaster recovery strategy for our catalog? Answer: "We employ a strict 3-2-1 backup strategy. Primary data is hosted on redundant storage arrays with RAID protection. We perform hourly incremental snapshots to a secondary local cluster for rapid rollback. Furthermore, encrypted, deduplicated backups are replicated daily to an immutable, offsite object storage provider (Backblaze B2) located in a geographically distinct region. Our Disaster Recovery Plan includes provisions for physical data shipment to ensure a Recovery Time Objective (RTO) of under 48 hours in the event of a total catastrophic failure."

Q3: How do you handle high traffic loads and ensure service availability? Answer: "Our infrastructure is built on a High Availability (HA) architecture. We utilize floating IP failover mechanisms and redundant load balancers to ensure zero downtime at the network edge. Our database layer employs a self-healing cluster topology (MariaDB Galera/Orchestrator) that guarantees data consistency and automatic failover in the event of a node loss. All critical services are decoupled and isolated within a private network, protected by strict firewall rules, ensuring that performance scales horizontally to meet demand without compromising security."
